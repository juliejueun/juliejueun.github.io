# Design System: Project Tangled (Personal Portfolio)

## 1. Overview
- **Concept:** A magical, whimsical, yet professional personal portfolio inspired by Disney's *Tangled*.
- **Vibe:** Warm glowing lights against a dark night sky, balancing creative expression with academic professionalism.

## 2. Color Palette
- **`Midnight Blue` (#121B38):** The primary background color, representing the night sky where the lanterns float.
- [cite_start]**`Royal Purple` (#6A0DAD):** Used for the navigation menu[cite: 598], headers, and card backgrounds to represent Rapunzel's dress.
- **`Glowing Gold` (#FFD700):** Accent color for glowing hover effects, links, and the Corona sun crest motifs.
- **`Starlight White` (#FDFDFF):** Main text color for high readability against the dark backgrounds.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings:** `Playfair Display` (Elegant and classic serif for a fairy-tale aesthetic).
- **Body Text:** `Lora` (Highly readable, slightly traditional but warm for the main content).

## 4. Required Content & Structure
To ensure all assignment requirements are met while maintaining the magical theme, the webpage will be structured with the following sections:

### Navigation
- [cite_start]A sticky top or side navigation menu linking to each section of the page[cite: 598].
- **Thematic touch:** The mobile hamburger menu icon (☰) can be styled to look like three strands of braided hair.

### Hero & About Me
- [cite_start]A profile photo or avatar framed by a faint golden glow[cite: 594].
- [cite_start]A personal introduction including your name, major, and a short self-introduction[cite: 593].
- **Thematic touch:** A subtle background animation using Canvas 2D API to show slowly rising, semi-transparent lanterns.

### CV / Resume
- [cite_start]A structured CV/resume section detailing your education, skills, and experience[cite: 596].
- **Thematic touch:** Information will be displayed on glassmorphism cards with a slight purple tint to let the lantern background shine through.

### Projects & Activities
- [cite_start]A showcase section highlighting at least two activities or projects[cite: 597].
- **Thematic touch:** Interactive project cards that float up slightly (TranslateY: -3px) with a `Glowing Gold` box-shadow when hovered over, mimicking a rising lantern.

### Future Courses
- [cite_start]A dedicated section listing the courses you plan to take in the future[cite: 599].
- **Thematic touch:** Use small Corona Sun Crest icons as bullet points for the list.

### Contact
- [cite_start]A section containing your contact information, such as your email, GitHub, or other links[cite: 595].

### AI Usage
- [cite_start]A short section at the bottom of the page detailing your AI Usage[cite: 615].
- [cite_start]This will include the name of the AI tool used (e.g., Replit Agent, Cursor, Claude Code) and one specific prompt you used to generate or modify the site[cite: 616].

## 5. UI Components
- **Dividers (`<hr>`):** Instead of standard lines, use thin, glowing gold lines.
- **Buttons:** `Royal Purple` background with smooth rounded corners (`border-radius: 25px`) and `Starlight White` text.
- **Loading Spinner:** A rotating Corona Sun Crest.
